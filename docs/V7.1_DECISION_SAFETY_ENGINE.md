# Trading AI Platform V7.1 — Decision & Safety Engine

## Objective
Balance performance and capital protection. The engine must not suppress valid opportunities merely because one data source is missing or one risk condition is elevated.

## Five brains
1. Technical Brain
2. Macro / News Brain
3. Risk / Regime Brain
4. Fundamental Brain
5. Statistical / Correlation Brain

## Dynamic weights
Weights change with the trading horizon:

- Intraday: Technical 38%, Macro/News 22%, Fundamental 8%, Statistical 32%
- Swing: Technical 28%, Macro/News 22%, Fundamental 27%, Statistical 23%
- Position: Technical 18%, Macro/News 25%, Fundamental 37%, Statistical 20%

If an optional brain lacks data, the available weights are re-normalized. Missing optional data does not automatically produce NO_TRADE.

## Risk architecture
Risk is not treated as a simple directional vote.

### Soft guards
Moderate drawdown, elevated volatility, high portfolio correlation, degraded feeds, imminent macro events, or elevated risk scores reduce position size and apply modest score penalties. They do not automatically block a high-quality opportunity.

### Hard veto
NO_TRADE is reserved for extreme or operationally unsafe states such as:
- broker disconnected
- invalid or stale market data
- daily loss >= 4%
- drawdown >= 10%
- volatility shock >= 4x baseline
- spread >= 4x baseline
- slippage >= 4x baseline

Thresholds are initial engineering defaults and must be calibrated by backtest, walk-forward and paper-trading evidence before live deployment.

## Decision bands
- BUY: score >= 66
- SELL: score <= 34
- WAIT: otherwise
- NO_TRADE: hard veto only

Position sizing is reduced by soft guards and confidence. A strong, high-confidence signal is allowed to retain meaningful exposure even when moderate soft guards are active.

## API
- `GET /decision/schema`
- `POST /statistical/evaluate`
- `POST /decision/evaluate`

The engine is a decision proposal layer. Live execution remains subject to broker integration, paper/shadow validation, risk limits and deployment controls.
